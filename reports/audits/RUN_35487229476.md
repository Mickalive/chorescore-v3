## 4. Technical soundness of each change

1. **30s TTL**: directly fixes the documented inert-cache defect. A successful dump is cached for 30s after completion; rapid successive calls share it. Failed dumps bypass the cache, so `waitFor` never spins on a stale failure. The 30s stale window on a *successful* negative dump adds at most 30s latency per element, absorbed by the widened windows.
2. **Invalidation on tap/swipe**: correct — every golden-path screen transition invalidates, so post-transition calls always get fresh state. `typeInto()`, `back()` and `swipeToTop()` do not invalidate but are **unused** in the golden path (latent, informational finding).
3. **dumpsys 12 → 3**: safe. The poll is a weak signal (package name can appear even when not foreground) and non-fatal; the real gate is `waitFor('Demarrer', 600000)`. Saves 45s of dead time before the window.
4. **Windows ≥ 60s**: strictly more tolerant than baseline. A 120s window fits 1-2 full dump attempts (60s dump + 30s cache + 60s dump); the 600s Demarrer window fits ~6-7 attempts. Total worst case stays well within the job timeout.
