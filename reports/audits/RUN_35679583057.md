- `waitFor()` computed `const globalGraceElapsed` **once before its loop**. After a force-stop inside the loop, the stale const keeps `inGlobalGrace = false` for the rest of the waitFor, leaving the relaunched app unprotected during its new cold start (documented up to 240s on API 35 x86_64).

The candidate fixes both: the dynamic grace re-activates the 450s cold-start window after every force-stop, and the 600s timeout gives the relaunched app enough time to cold-start and render the groups list.

## 3. Correctness of the dynamic grace

- `globalThis._appLaunchTime` is always initialized: `waitFor()` sets it to `Date.now()` if undefined (line 497), `launch()` sets it after the warm-up (line 946), and every force-stop handler (app-ANR, crash detector, stuck detector) resets it. No undefined access is possible.
- The dynamic computation is strictly more correct than the const: after any force-stop + relaunch, the app needs a fresh cold-start window, and the grace should re-activate. The waitFor timeout still bounds the total wait, so a genuinely stuck app cannot be protected indefinitely.
- The `inPerWaitGrace` overlay (`elapsedMs < graceMs`) is unchanged; the `if (inPerWaitGrace || inGlobalGrace)` gate and the `sinceLastForceStop >= FORCE_STOP_RECOVERY_MS` recovery guard are unchanged.
