### 2. Sequence accumulation — fixed

`validateSettlementPreconditions` gained a `priorSettlements: CrossLedgerSettlement[] = []` parameter, threaded into both `calculateContributionBalances` and `calculateFinancialBalances`. `validateSettlementSequence` passes `appliedSettlements` so each subsequent settlement is checked against balances that include all earlier settlements.

Independent execution:
- a has 15 min credit, b +1500; s1 consumes 15 min / 500 CHF (all credit), s2 consumes 10 min / 333 CHF → `valid=false`, error names `Settlement[1]`.
- a has 30 min credit, b +1500; s1 15/500 + s2 10/333 → `valid=true`, no errors.

The accumulation test discriminates the fix: against a money-fixed-but-no-accumulation variant, s2 would pass (a still shows 15 credit) and the test would fail.
