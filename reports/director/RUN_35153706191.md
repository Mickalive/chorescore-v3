However, two must-fix findings prevent acceptance:

### MF-1 — Unified history pagination is incorrect

The two-repo cursor logic in `app/(tabs)/add.tsx` (`loadHistory` + `activityEntries` merge) advances the cursor to the NEWER of the two repo cursors, causing the stream with the older cursor to re-fetch entries already shown on the previous page (duplicate rows in the merged view). Additionally, the merged view is hard-capped at `PAGE_SIZE*10=150` entries, so older loaded history is unreachable while "Charger plus" keeps appearing.

**Required fix:** Implement a single merged cursor-based pagination across both contribution and expense streams (one cursor keyed to the merged `occurredAt` ordering), remove the hard 150-entry cap, and add tests covering the two-repo merged pagination path.

### MF-2 — Contribution form missing constitutionally-required features

The contribution form does not surface PersistentTask shortcuts (persistentTaskId is always null despite a demo fixture seeding "Vaisselle"), date/heure selection (occurredAt is always `todayLocal()`), or the share action (history actions only expose Edit/Suppr). Constitution §5A and the V3-03 outcome explicitly require all three.

**Required fix:** Add PersistentTask shortcuts (prefill label/value/beneficiaries), a date/heure picker for occurredAt, and a share action wired to the share port, keeping the form compact and chrono-free.
