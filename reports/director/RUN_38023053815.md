## What the accepted delta delivers

The delta hardens the E2E golden path against transient emulator infrastructure failure **without weakening any
assertion**:

- **UI — Groups root** — `app/index.tsx` adds `testID="groups.createButton"` to the primary create action.
- **UI — Button** — `src/ui/components/Button.tsx` accepts and forwards `testID` to the native `TouchableOpacity`
  (resource-id), so the E2E driver targets the control directly instead of a localized label.
- **E2E script** — `scripts/e2e-android.js` introduces `openCreateGroupForm()`: drives the stable
  `groups.createButton` testID, retries the tap, clears transient infra ANR overlays between attempts, polls for the
  real form field `groups.nameInput` until it appears (240s timeout), and falls back to the localized label where a
  testID is absent. The assertion is intact — the form must still open or the golden path fails. The old non-waiting
  pattern `tapLabel('Créer un groupe') + waitFor('Créer')` (which matched the button itself and never waited for the
  form) is removed.
- **Test coverage** — `__tests__/domain/v3-08-finalizer-wrapper.test.ts` adds a test verifying the stable testID, the
  retrying helper, and the removal of the fragile pattern.

No app ANR is masked: real ANRs still force-stop + relaunch. No product assertion, ledger, sync, analytics, privacy,
theme, chrono or premium behavior was changed.
